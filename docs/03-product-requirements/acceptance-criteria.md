# Acceptance Criteria

**Status:** Draft for founder approval

## Tenant and authorization

- A user from Tenant A cannot read, change, download, or infer Tenant B
  records through any supported resource.
- Branch-scoped users cannot access unauthorized branch records.
- Technician access is limited to assigned jobs.
- Exceptional support access is authorized, time-limited where appropriate, and
  audited.

## Inquiry and booking

- Staff can record an inquiry from every supported source channel.
- Staff can find or create a customer without creating unintended duplicates.
- Required contact, address, service, and preferred-date data is validated.
- Inquiry status and follow-up activity are visible.
- An approved inquiry can become a booking and job without re-entering core
  customer information.
- Conflicting technician assignments are detected and handled explicitly.

## Quotation and job

- Staff can create standard-price and quotation-based AC-cleaning requests.
- Customer approval is recorded with time and actor or secure-link evidence.
- Approved quotations retain their prior contents through revisions.
- Technicians can update permitted jobs from an ordinary Android phone.
- Completion submissions support notes, photos, materials, and findings.
- Authorized reviewers can accept or return submissions for correction.

## Invoicing and payment

- Authorized staff can create an invoice from confirmed work.
- Cash, GCash, and bank-transfer payments can be recorded with amount, method,
  date, reference, and proof where applicable.
- Partial payments produce the correct remaining balance.
- Payment status cannot be marked fully paid before the outstanding balance is
  satisfied.
- Job status, invoice status, and payment status remain independently
  traceable.

## Customer links and reporting

- A customer link exposes only its intended resource.
- Tokens are unguessable and expired links are rejected.
- Reports reconcile to invoice and payment records.
- No customer link exposes business administration or another customer.

## Pilot acceptance

- A pilot can establish a pre-pilot missed-inquiry baseline.
- All pilot inquiries can be recorded regardless of source channel.
- Follow-up status can be measured consistently over 30 days.
- The pilot can calculate whether missed or forgotten inquiries declined by at
  least 50% against baseline.
