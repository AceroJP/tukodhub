# MVP Scope

**Status:** Draft for founder approval

## Included

1. Tenant registration and authentication.
2. Business profile and one-branch setup.
3. Up to four users in the introductory pricing hypothesis.
4. Owner, administrator, dispatcher, and technician permissions.
5. Customer records and service history.
6. Staff-entered inquiries from Messenger, phone, SMS, and walk-ins.
7. AC-cleaning-first service catalog.
8. Inquiry follow-up and status tracking.
9. Booking calendar and technician assignment.
10. Standard-price and quotation paths.
11. Job orders and valid status transitions.
12. Android-friendly technician updates.
13. Notes, service photos, materials, and additional findings.
14. Office review and completion confirmation.
15. Basic invoicing.
16. Cash, GCash, and bank-transfer payment recording.
17. Basic operational and financial reports.
18. Optional secure, expiring customer links.
19. Tenant isolation, branch authorization, and audit records.

## Secondary but not launch-critical

- Public customer booking page.
- Additional service types beyond AC cleaning.
- Additional branches and expanded user limits.

## Excluded from MVP

- Online payment gateway collection.
- Full customer accounts and portal.
- Offline synchronization.
- Advanced inventory management.
- Automated SMS integration.
- Advanced analytics.
- Recurring maintenance automation.
- Native mobile applications.
- AI-powered assistance.

## MVP boundary rule

Any feature that adds complexity without improving inquiry capture, booking
coordination, technician visibility, completion verification, or payment
recording should be deferred until pilot evidence supports it.
