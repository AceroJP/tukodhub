## Approval

The founder approved the current Phase 0 hypothesis package and authorized
TukodHub to proceed to Phase 1 - Market and Competitor Research.

## Important qualification

This approval authorizes the phase transition. The customer segment, problem,
workflow, value outcome, pricing, adoption risk, and pilot criteria remain
market hypotheses unless supported by interviews or pilot evidence.

## Phase 1 boundary

Phase 1 must research direct competitors, indirect alternatives, competitor
features, pricing, underserved needs, differentiation, and relevant
open-source projects with verified licenses. Phase 1 requires a separate
founder approval before Phase 2 begins.
