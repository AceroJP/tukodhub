# Competitor Analysis

**Phase:** Phase 1 - Market and Competitor Research  
**Status:** Initial desk research; founder review required  
**Research date:** 2026-11-10

## Market context

The initial TukodHub segment is small AC installation and repair businesses in
Malaybalay City and nearby Bukidnon. The research did not establish a dominant
local AC-specific SaaS product. The practical competitive set is therefore:

1. Global field-service platforms.
2. General CRM and appointment tools.
3. Manual and familiar communication tools already used by the target segment.

The absence of a clearly identified local specialist is not proof that no local
competitor exists. Local discovery interviews and searches of Philippine
business groups are still required.

## Direct competitors

### Jobber

Jobber targets contractors and provides customer management, scheduling,
quoting, invoicing, payments, customer communication, photos, reporting, and
workflow features. Its official pricing page presents plans based on team size,
with annual-billing prices substantially above TukodHub's ₱499 hypothesis for
many teams.

**Threat:** Strong all-in-one contractor workflow and established brand.  
**Weakness for the initial segment:** Foreign pricing, broader complexity, and
no demonstrated Bukidnon-specific workflow or payment-recording focus.

Source: [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/)

### Housecall Pro

Housecall Pro presents online booking, invoices and payments, scheduling and
dispatching, quotes and proposals, and review management. Its plan selection
depends on industry, team size, and business goals.

**Threat:** Directly overlaps booking, dispatch, quotes, and payments.  
**Weakness for the initial segment:** Pricing is configured through a
recommendation flow, and the product is positioned for a broader market than
small Bukidnon AC teams.

Source: [Housecall Pro pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)

### Tradify

Tradify provides job management, scheduling, quoting, invoicing, material
markups, payments, customer communications, job photos, recurring work, and
reports. Its official pricing page lists per-user USD plans.

**Threat:** Strong match for trade contractors and field job execution.  
**Weakness for the initial segment:** Per-user foreign-currency pricing can be
misaligned with a ₱499-per-business hypothesis, and local operational context
is not its stated differentiator.

Source: [Tradify pricing](https://www.tradifyhq.com/pricing)

## Indirect competitors and substitutes

### Bigin by Zoho

Bigin is a general CRM with contacts, pipelines, activities, automation,
mobile apps, booking pages, forms, dashboards, and integrations. It can
organize inquiries but does not appear to be a complete AC field-service
workflow out of the box.

Source: [Bigin pricing and features](https://www.bigin.com/pricing.html)

### Existing manual stack

Facebook Messenger, phone calls, SMS, notebooks, spreadsheets, GCash, and bank
transfers are not software competitors in the conventional sense, but they are
the strongest adoption substitute. They are familiar, immediately available,
and require no new subscription or training.

### General accounting or ERP suites

Broad ERP and accounting products can cover invoicing, customers, and
inventory, but may impose more setup and process complexity than a small
owner-operated AC business wants. They should be evaluated as alternatives,
not assumed to be suitable competitors without customer evidence.

## Initial conclusion

TukodHub should not compete by matching every feature of global field-service
platforms. The initial opportunity is a lower-friction, Philippine-context
workflow for capturing existing-channel inquiries, coordinating AC cleaning
jobs, supporting ordinary Android phones, and recording local payment methods.
