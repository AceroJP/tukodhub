# Phase 1 Market Research Summary

**Phase:** Phase 1 - Market and Competitor Research  
**Status:** Initial research completed; founder approval required  
**Research date:** 2026-11-10

## Findings

- The main competitive threat is a combination of global field-service
  platforms and familiar manual tools, rather than one confirmed local AC SaaS
  leader.
- Jobber, Housecall Pro, and Tradify demonstrate that scheduling, dispatch,
  quotes, job management, invoicing, payments, and field documentation are
  established software categories.
- Bigin demonstrates that a lower-cost general CRM can cover contacts,
  pipelines, forms, booking pages, and dashboards, but it does not represent a
  complete AC field-service workflow without configuration.
- Manual channels remain the most important substitute because they are
  familiar, already adopted, and free or low cost.
- Global pricing is commonly in USD and may be per user or substantially above
  the ₱499 TukodHub hypothesis. This creates a possible affordability angle but
  does not prove demand.
- OpenFieldService is a candidate MIT-licensed project for further technical
  review. Odoo and ERPNext are relevant open-source references but use LGPLv3
  and GPLv3 respectively, not MIT.

## Recommended market position

Focus the next validation round on whether small Bukidnon AC businesses value:

1. Staff-entered inquiry tracking from existing channels.
2. AC-cleaning-first booking and scheduling simplicity.
3. Android-friendly technician updates with office review.
4. Philippine payment recording for cash, GCash, and bank transfer.
5. A simple business-level price near ₱499/month.

## Remaining research gaps

- No verified interviews with target businesses yet.
- No confirmed local competitor or local pricing benchmark.
- No measured baseline for forgotten inquiries.
- No willingness-to-pay evidence.
- No hands-on trials of competitors.
- No complete dependency, issue-history, or security review of open-source
  candidates.
