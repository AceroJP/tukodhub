# Pricing Comparison

**Research date:** 2026-11-10  
**Status:** Public-price snapshot; prices and plan contents can change

| Product | Public pricing signal | Pricing implication |
|---|---|---|
| TukodHub | Hypothesis: ₱499 per business/month, up to 4 users and 1 branch | Low, simple business-level entry price |
| Jobber | Official page advertises plans from $29/month and shows team-size plans reaching hundreds of USD/month | Often materially higher than the TukodHub hypothesis; comparison must account for currency and included users |
| Tradify | Official page lists Lite at $47/user/month, Pro at $51/user/month, and Plus at $61/user/month | Per-user pricing can become expensive for a 4-user team |
| Housecall Pro | Official page uses industry, team size, and goals to recommend a plan | Exact price requires an interactive sales/pricing flow |
| Bigin | Official page lists Free and paid plans such as Express at US$9/user/month monthly or US$7/user/month billed yearly | Lower-cost CRM alternative, but field-service workflow fit is incomplete |

## Pricing interpretation

The ₱499 hypothesis is not validated merely because global alternatives cost
more. TukodHub must prove that:

- The target business experiences a costly enough problem.
- The product saves more time or recovers enough work to justify the fee.
- Four users and one branch match actual small-business usage.
- Support, onboarding, hosting, storage, and communication costs do not make the
  plan unprofitable.
