# Differentiation Strategy

**Phase:** Phase 1 - Market and Competitor Research  
**Status:** Initial strategy; founder review required

## Proposed positioning

TukodHub should be positioned as:

> A simple, affordable operations workspace for small Philippine AC service
> businesses that turns Messenger, phone, SMS, and walk-in requests into
> organized jobs, technician updates, and reliable payment records.

## Potential differentiators

1. **Existing-channel-first workflow**  
   Staff can capture inquiries without forcing customers to use a new portal or
   changing how they communicate.

2. **AC cleaning-first simplicity**  
   The initial workflow can be optimized for a specific service instead of
   exposing a large generic FSM configuration surface.

3. **Philippine operating context**  
   Support for Philippine pesos, GCash and bank-transfer recording, local
   address realities, and ordinary Android phones should be validated and made
   central where customers value them.

4. **Business-level affordability**  
   The ₱499 hypothesis is simpler to understand than foreign per-user pricing,
   but willingness to pay and unit economics remain unproven.

5. **Controlled field-to-office workflow**  
   Technicians submit mobile evidence; owners or dispatchers retain authority to
   confirm completion, invoice, and record payments.

## Differentiators not yet proven

These are positioning hypotheses, not defensible advantages until competitors
and customers confirm them:

- Local pricing is meaningfully more accessible.
- Bukidnon-specific support is valuable enough to drive adoption.
- Existing-channel capture is better than requiring online booking.
- AC cleaning specialization is preferable to a general contractor platform.

## Strategic recommendation

Do not compete on feature count. Compete on time-to-value, ease of adoption,
local workflow fit, and a measurable reduction in forgotten inquiries. Test the
positioning with at least five target-business interviews and a structured
pilot.
