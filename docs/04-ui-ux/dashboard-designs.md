# Dashboard Designs

**Status:** Draft for founder approval

## Business dashboard

### Primary purpose

Help the owner or dispatcher answer: What needs attention today?

### Information hierarchy

1. Follow-ups due and new inquiries.
2. Today's bookings and technician assignments.
3. Jobs awaiting completion review.
4. Unpaid and partially paid invoices.
5. Secondary trend summaries.

### Recommended widgets

- New inquiries today and this week.
- Follow-ups overdue.
- Today's schedule.
- Jobs in progress.
- Jobs awaiting review.
- Outstanding balance.
- Recent payments.

Statistics must use real tenant data. Empty or insufficient data should be
shown honestly rather than replaced with fake numbers.

## Technician dashboard

### Primary purpose

Help technicians start the correct job and submit complete evidence quickly.

### Recommended content

- Today’s assigned jobs.
- Next appointment.
- Jobs awaiting submission or correction.
- Clear status actions.
- Profile and availability.

Avoid financial metrics and unrelated administration.

## Platform dashboard

### Primary purpose

Help platform administrators monitor tenants and platform health without
automatically exposing tenant business records.

### Recommended widgets

- Active tenants.
- Trial or subscription status.
- Suspended accounts.
- System health.
- Recent audited support access.

## Responsive dashboard behavior

- Desktop: persistent navigation and multi-column cards.
- Tablet: collapsible navigation and two-column content.
- Mobile: prioritized single-column queue, schedule, and action cards.
