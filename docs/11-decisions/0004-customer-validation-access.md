# Decision 0004: Customer Validation Access

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Outreach target; not yet validated

## Current access

The founder does not yet have confirmed relationships or partnerships with AC
business owners in the target market.

## Initial outreach target

Attempt to identify and contact 5-10 small AC installation and repair business
owners or operators in Malaybalay City and nearby areas. Potential channels are
personal introductions, referrals, Facebook business pages, local community
groups, direct inquiries, and visits to local providers.

The initial goal is to complete at least five discovery interviews within
2-3 weeks, subject to business-owner availability and the founder's academic
schedule.

## Discovery implication

No product or pricing assumption should be treated as validated until direct
customer conversations provide supporting evidence. Interview recruitment is
itself an immediate Phase 0 activity.
