# Decision 0012: Initial Pilot Success Criterion

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Hypothesis; requires baseline and pilot evidence

## Criterion

During a 30-day pilot, TukodHub should reduce missed or forgotten customer
inquiries by at least 50% compared with the participating business's baseline
before using the product.

## Measurement method

1. Establish the business's pre-pilot baseline.
2. Record every incoming inquiry during the pilot, regardless of whether it
   arrives through Messenger, phone, SMS, or a walk-in.
3. Track inquiry status and follow-up activity.
4. Compare missed or forgotten inquiries at the end of the pilot.

For example, if 10 of 40 baseline inquiries received no follow-up, the pilot
target is 5 or fewer missed inquiries.

## Validation note

The 50% target is an initial success hypothesis. Baseline quality, consistent
recording, and pilot evidence are required before treating it as a reliable
measure of product value.
