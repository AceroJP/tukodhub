# Decision 0003: Provisional Service Workflow

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Provisional workflow; pending customer validation

## Workflow hypothesis

A typical AC service request may progress from multi-channel inquiry, customer
and equipment information collection, availability checking, quotation,
customer approval, technician assignment, field service execution, completion
confirmation, payment, and record keeping.

## Expected participants and tools

Participants may include the customer, owner or dispatcher, technician, and an
optional receptionist or administrator. Current tools may include Facebook
Messenger, phone calls, SMS, notebooks, spreadsheets, GCash, and bank transfers.

## Discovery implication

The initial product hypothesis is to centralize this lifecycle, but each step
must be validated with actual businesses. In particular, discovery should test
where information is lost, who performs each step, how exceptions are handled,
and which records businesses are required to keep.
