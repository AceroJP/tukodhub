# Decision 0002: Primary Problem Hypothesis

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Hypothesis; pending customer validation

## Problem hypothesis

Small AC service businesses may have difficulty managing customer requests,
bookings, and technician schedules in one organized system. Requests often
arrive through multiple channels, while customer details and appointments may
be recorded manually in notebooks or spreadsheets.

## Potential impact

The suspected consequences are missed inquiries, double bookings, delayed
responses, poor visibility into pending and completed jobs, lost follow-ups,
and wasted technician-coordination time. These may lead to lost customers,
lower productivity, and missed revenue.

## Evidence status

This is based on the founder's current assumption and has not yet been verified
with business owners. No reliable statistics are currently available for
frequency, severity, or financial loss.

## Discovery implication

Customer interviews must test whether this is a frequent and expensive problem,
how businesses handle it today, and which part of the workflow causes the most
harm.
