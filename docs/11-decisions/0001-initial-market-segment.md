# Decision 0001: Initial Market Segment

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Initial hypothesis; pending market validation

## Decision

Prioritize small, independent air-conditioning installation and repair
businesses in Malaybalay City and nearby areas in Bukidnon, Philippines, for
initial customer discovery.

The intended business size is approximately 1-10 technicians, including
owner-operated teams. Initial services include AC installation, cleaning,
preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair.

CCTV/security-system providers and other service businesses are deferred as
future expansion segments.

## Rationale

This segment is narrow enough to investigate a specific operating workflow and
is geographically accessible for interviews. These businesses are hypothesized
to rely on Facebook Messenger, phone calls, spreadsheets, notebooks, or other
manual tools for daily operations.

## Consequences

- Discovery interviews should prioritize actual AC business owners or operators
  in the target area.
- The MVP should not yet be designed to support every service industry.
- The hypothesis must be tested for operational pain, willingness to pay, and
  workflow similarity before finalizing product scope.
