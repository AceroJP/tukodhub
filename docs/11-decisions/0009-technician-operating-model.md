# Decision 0009: Technician Operating Model

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Founder decision; pending field validation

## Decision

Use a hybrid workflow in which technicians update job details directly from
their phones, while the owner or dispatcher reviews and finalizes the work.

## Technician capabilities

Technicians should be able to:

- View assigned jobs and schedules.
- Update status through Assigned, In Progress, and Work Completed.
- Add work notes.
- Upload before-and-after service photos.
- Record materials or parts used.
- Report additional problems or recommended repairs.

## Office review and authorization

Owners or dispatchers should monitor progress, review submitted details and
photos, request corrections, verify reported work, confirm completion, and
proceed with invoicing and payment recording. Technicians must not finalize
invoices, record business payments, or permanently close jobs without the
appropriate authorization.

## MVP boundary

The interface must be mobile-friendly and usable on ordinary Android
smartphones. Reliable online updates are prioritized; offline synchronization
is deferred.
