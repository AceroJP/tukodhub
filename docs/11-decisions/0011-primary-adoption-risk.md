# Decision 0011: Primary Adoption Risk

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Hypothesis; pending interviews and pilot testing

## Risk

Small AC businesses may resist adopting TukodHub because learning the system
and changing existing routines could feel like additional work. Users are
already familiar with Messenger, phone calls, SMS, notebooks, and spreadsheets.

## Product implications

The MVP should prioritize:

- Simple, understandable interfaces.
- Mobile-friendly technician screens.
- Minimal steps for inquiries and bookings.
- Easy customer and job record management.
- Clear navigation without unnecessary features.
- Short onboarding and practical guidance.

## Validation requirements

Interviews and pilots must test whether users can complete core tasks quickly,
whether encoding creates perceived extra work, and whether the product reduces
more effort than it introduces.
