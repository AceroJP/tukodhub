# Decision 0005: Value Outcome Hypothesis

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Hypothesis; pending interviews and pilot evidence

## Proposed customer outcome

TukodHub should help small AC businesses increase successfully completed and
paid service jobs by preventing missed inquiries, delayed follow-ups, and
poorly coordinated bookings.

## Initial measurable hypothesis

A business receiving approximately 40 inquiries per month might recover 3-5
additional paid jobs per month with better inquiry tracking, booking
management, and follow-up.

## Validation requirements

This outcome must be tested with actual businesses. Discovery and pilot
validation should establish the baseline number of inquiries, current losses,
average job revenue and profit, and whether the product causes a measurable
improvement. The result must be sufficient to justify the subscription cost.
