# Decision 0006: Initial Pricing Hypothesis

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Hypothesis; pending willingness-to-pay validation

## Proposed introductory plan

Charge **₱499 per business per month** with no per-job fee.

The plan would include:

- Up to four users.
- One business location or branch.
- Customer management and service history.
- Customer inquiry and booking management.
- Quotations and job orders.
- Technician assignment and job status tracking.
- Basic invoicing and payment recording.
- Basic business dashboard and reports.

Additional branches, users, advanced inventory management, SMS notifications,
and advanced reporting may be offered in higher plans later.

## Validation requirements

Customer interviews and pilot testing must determine whether the target
businesses can afford ₱499, whether the included features address a valuable
problem, whether the recovered-job outcome justifies the price, and whether the
support and operating costs make the plan commercially viable.
