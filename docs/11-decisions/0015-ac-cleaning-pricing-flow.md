# Decision 0015: AC Cleaning Pricing Flow

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Phase:** Phase 0 - Product Discovery  
**Status:** Provisional; validation details unresolved

## Decision

AC cleaning does not have one assumed pricing flow. Depending on the request,
the MVP may need to support both a standard-price booking path and an
assessment or quotation path.

## Unresolved discovery question

Customer interviews must identify which factors change the price or require
assessment, such as AC unit type, number of units, location, condition,
accessibility, or additional work.
